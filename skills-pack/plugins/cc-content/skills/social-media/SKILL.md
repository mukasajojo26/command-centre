# Social Media

## Step 0: Voice and pillars (once)

Create or load `social-profile.md`:
```markdown
Who I am: <role, expertise, region>
Audience: <who should follow, what they need>
Goal: <hiring visibility / leads / authority / community>
Pillars (3-4): e.g. 1) Hotel security operations 2) AI and automation in safety 3) Leadership lessons 4) Career growth in UAE
Voice: <3 adjectives>. Avoid: <words>.
Proof bank: <real stories, numbers, certifications the user has shared>
```
Never invent personal stories, numbers, employers or credentials. Ask, or leave a `[YOUR STORY: ...]` slot.

## Platform specs (verify periodically, platforms change limits)

| Platform | Best formats | Length guide | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Text post, document carousel (PDF), short video | Hook in first 2 lines (~210 chars before "see more"), 900-1,500 chars total | Links in post body reduce reach; put them in a comment if the user prefers reach |
| X | Single post, thread | 280 chars per post (more with Premium) | First post must stand alone |
| Instagram | Carousel (up to 20), Reels | Caption hook first 125 chars | 3-5 relevant hashtags |
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 15-60 s vertical video | Hook in first 2 s | On-screen text plus captions |
| Facebook | Native video, image + text | Short | Groups often outperform pages |

## Post formula (LinkedIn default)

1. **Hook** (1-2 lines): a specific claim, number, or tension. "We cut false alarms by 40% without new cameras."
2. **Context**: the situation in 2-3 short lines.
3. **Substance**: 3-5 lines or a short list of what was done and why.
4. **Takeaway**: one line the reader can apply.
5. **Prompt**: a specific question, not "Thoughts?".

## Workflows

**Single post**: ask pillar + idea + proof. Write 2 hooks and 1 body. Run the `humanizer` skill rules (no dashes, no buzzwords).

**Repurpose**: from one source (article, report, talk transcript) produce: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 carousel outline (8-10 slides: title, 1 idea per slide, summary, CTA), 1 X thread (5-7 posts), 1 short-video script (hook, 3 beats, CTA, with on-screen text).

**Calendar**: 4-week table: Date | Platform | Pillar | Format | Hook | Status. 3 posts/week is a sustainable default. Rotate pillars. Mark 1 in 4 posts as promotional at most.

**Review**: user pastes analytics (impressions, engagement rate, follows, profile views). Report top 3 and bottom 3 posts, the pattern behind them, and 3 changes for next month. Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + reposts) / impressions.

## Guardrails
- Confidentiality: no client names, site layouts, guard schedules, CCTV stills, incident details or anything that could aid an attacker. Flag such content before drafting.
- No engagement bait ("comment YES"), no fake urgency.
- Respect employer social media policies and UAE cybercrime law on defamation and privacy (Federal Decree-Law 34 of 2021).
